Sales Performance Analysis

This project focuses on analyzing sales data to understand revenue distribution, sales trends, and business performance across different categories, products, and regions. The goal is to support data-driven business decisions.

Tools Used

  • Excel – Initial data cleaning and summaries
  • Python – Data analysis and visualization
  • Matplotlib – Charts and plots

Revenue by Category

Electronics contribute the majority of total revenue, followed by Furniture, while Office supplies generate the least revenue. This indicates a strong demand for electronic products.

Monthly Sales Trend

Sales were higher in January compared to February, showing a declining trend. This insight can help businesses investigate seasonal or operational factors affecting sales.

Product-wise Revenue

Laptops and mobile devices generate the highest revenue, while products such as chairs, desks, and printers contribute significantly less. High-performing products should be prioritized in inventory planning.

Region-wise Revenue

The North region generates the highest revenue, followed by West, South, and East. This indicates regional performance differences and potential areas for market expansion.

Business Recommendations

  • Focus marketing strategies on high-performing regions
  • Increase stock availability for top-selling products
  • Investigate reasons for lower sales in underperforming regions
